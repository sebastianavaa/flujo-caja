Small rounded pill that labels a status or category. Tones mirror the product's installment states.

```jsx
<Badge tone="active">Activa</Badge>
<Badge tone="paid">Pagada</Badge>
<Badge tone="future">Futura</Badge>
<Badge tone="role">Implementation Engineer</Badge>
```

Tones: `active` (blue), `paid` (green), `future` (yellow), `danger` (red), `role` (accent fill), `neutral`. Keep labels one or two words.
